Privacy Policy

Mekax OS · Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Türkçe

Mekax OS ("the app") is an internal marketing production tool operated by Mekax Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. It covers Mekax OS only; each of our games has its own privacy policy.

Summary

  • Mekax OS uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel, you agree to this policy and to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • The app is available only to our team. It uploads videos as private, and a video becomes public only after a team member approves it.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.
  • You can disconnect a channel and revoke access at any time.

Information from Google and YouTube

When you connect a YouTube channel, the app receives and stores:

  • Authorization tokens that let the app act for the channel within the permissions you gave. They are encrypted before being stored.
  • Channel details: channel name, channel ID, channel handle and the ID of the channel's uploads list.
  • Upload results: the ID of each video uploaded through the app, and its privacy and processing status.

To avoid duplicate uploads, the app may check the details of up to 50 of the channel's most recent uploads, including their tags, in order to find a video it uploaded. It uses these details for that comparison and does not store them.

The app can make public or delete only videos it uploaded, and only when a team member confirms it. It does not read or store comments, subscribers, watch history, analytics or your Google account password.

How we use it

We use this information only to show which channel is connected, upload the videos you choose, check their status, make a video public or delete it when a team member confirms it, and report errors. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not use this information to train artificial intelligence models.

Information you enter

The app sends the video you choose to YouTube together with the title, description, tags, category and made-for-kids declaration you provide, and it keeps a record of them.

Team accounts and technical records

To let team members sign in, the app stores their email address and display name. It also keeps session records, a record of important actions such as connecting a channel or uploading a video, and technical logs used to fix problems.

Cookies and browser storage

The app uses cookies only to keep you signed in and to protect sign-in and channel connection requests. Your browser also remembers which product you last selected. The app shows no ads and uses no third-party advertising or analytics cookies.

Sharing

We share information with Google and YouTube only as needed to provide these features, for example when uploading a video. We do not transfer it to anyone else unless required by law, needed for security, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, and then only with your prior explicit consent where the Google API Services User Data Policy requires it. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

Data is stored on a computer operated by Mekax Studio, including in its backups. Authorization tokens and upload session addresses are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and they do not appear in the app's screens or logs. The app connects to Google over HTTPS. Google stores videos and channel data on its own servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to our team.

How long we keep it

  • Authorization tokens: until you disconnect the channel, when they are deleted immediately from the app's active database. Copies in older encrypted backups remain until those backups are deleted.
  • Channel details and upload records: kept as a record of past actions until you ask us to delete them. We will delete them as soon as possible and within 7 calendar days of your request.
  • Team account records and logs: kept while they are needed to operate and secure the app, or until you ask us to delete them where the law allows.

Videos already uploaded stay on YouTube until you delete them there. See Data Deletion.

Revoking access

You can disconnect a channel on the app's Social Media Accounts screen, which also asks Google to revoke the app's access. You can also revoke it at any time in your Google security settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. For any request, question or complaint, email us at the address below.

Children

Mekax OS is a tool for our team and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
