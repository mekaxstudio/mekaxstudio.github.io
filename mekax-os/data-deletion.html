Data Deletion

Mekax OS · Effective date: November 3, 2026

This page explains how to remove a connected account's data from Mekax OS.

1. Disconnect the channel

On the app's Publishing screen, choose Disconnect. The app immediately deletes the channel's authorization tokens from its active database and asks Google to revoke its access.

You can also revoke access yourself in your Google security settings. Revoking there stops the app's access to your channel; to delete the records described below as well, send the request in step 2.

2. Ask us to delete the remaining records

Email {{ site.email }} from an address we can link to the account, and include the channel name or channel ID. We will delete the channel details and upload records held by the app as soon as possible and within 7 calendar days of your request, and we will confirm by email. Copies in backups are deleted when those backups are removed.

What is not deleted this way

Videos already uploaded stay on YouTube. To remove them, delete them in YouTube Studio.
