Mekax OS

Mekax OS is Mekax Studio's internal tool for making marketing videos from our own games and publishing them to our own YouTube channels and, soon, TikTok accounts.

What it does

  • Records gameplay. It records real gameplay of our games, such as Color Sort.
  • Makes videos. It turns those recordings into vertical videos with our game's logo, a call to action and an end screen, and writes their titles and descriptions in several languages.
  • Uploads and publishes after approval. A team member connects our own YouTube channel. Uploads start as private, and the app asks a team member for approval in the app or in our team's Telegram chat. A video becomes public only after an authorized team member approves it, and a team member can delete a video the app uploaded, after confirming.
  • TikTok (being added). Through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API, the app will send an approved video to one of our TikTok accounts, either as a draft in its TikTok inbox or, once TikTok allows it, as a post with the settings the team member chooses.

Who can use it

Mekax OS is not offered to the public. It runs on a computer operated by Mekax Studio and only our team can sign in.

Why it asks for access to YouTube

Mekax OS uses YouTube API Services. When a channel is connected, it asks Google for three permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the video you choose to your channel.
  • See, edit and permanently delete your videos (youtube.force-ssl): only to change a video the app uploaded from private to public, or to delete it, each after a team member confirms it. The app does not edit or delete anything else.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to show which channel is connected, and to check that an uploaded video exists, its visibility and that it has finished processing.

Mekax OS uses this access only for these purposes. It does not read comments, subscribers, watch history or analytics.

Why it asks for access to TikTok

When the TikTok connection is switched on, Mekax OS will ask TikTok for user.info.basic (to show which account is connected), video.upload (to send an approved video to the account's inbox as a draft) and, after TikTok's review, video.publish (to post an approved video directly). It will not read followers, messages, comments or analytics. See the Privacy Policy.
