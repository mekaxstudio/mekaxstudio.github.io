Mekax OS

Mekax OS is Mekax Studio's internal tool for making marketing videos from our own games and uploading them to our YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Records gameplay. It records real gameplay of our games, such as Color Sort.
  • Makes creatives. It produces vertical marketing videos from those recordings, with or without a short opening text.
  • Uploads and publishes. A team member can connect a YouTube channel and upload a chosen video with its title, description and tags. Uploads start as private. A video becomes public only after an authorized team member approves it, and a team member can delete a video the app uploaded, after confirming.

Who can use it

Mekax OS is not offered to the public. It runs on a computer operated by Mekax Studio and only our team can sign in.

Why it asks for access to YouTube

Mekax OS uses YouTube API Services. When a channel is connected, it asks Google for three permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the video you choose to your channel.
  • See, edit and permanently delete your videos (youtube.force-ssl): only to change a video the app uploaded from private to public, or to delete it, each after a team member confirms it. The app does not edit or delete anything else.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to show which channel is connected, and to check that an uploaded video exists, its visibility and that it has finished processing.

Mekax OS uses this access only for these purposes. It does not read comments, subscribers, watch history or analytics.
