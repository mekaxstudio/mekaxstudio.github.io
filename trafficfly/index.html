Traffic Fly

Weave through highway traffic, charge your rocket with near misses and flip over the car ahead.

Drive as far as you can through busy traffic. Every near miss charges your rocket; when it is full, your car lifts off, rolls over the car in front and lands back on the road. Play the Highway, Head-On and Rocket Mission modes, collect 13 cars and make them your own with paint, rims and upgrades.

Contact us

Found a bug or have an idea? Email mekaxstudio@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the rocket?

Buy it in the garage under the rocket tab. Each rocket level needs one near miss fewer to charge. In Rocket Missions every car gets a rocket for free, and before a game you can watch a short video to try the rocket once.

What counts as a near miss?

Passing close beside another car at speed without touching it.

How do I change the controls?

Settings lets you steer by swiping, with buttons, by tilting the phone or with a thumb stick, and turn automatic gas on or off.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your money, cars and records.

Are there ads or purchases?

The game is free and shows ads (Google AdMob), no banners. You can buy "Remove Ads", "Double Money" and money packs in the shop; "Restore purchases" brings back Remove Ads and Double Money on a new device. See the Privacy Policy.
