Privacy Policy

WearUp · Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Türkçe

WearUp ("the app") is developed and published by Mekax Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who processes it and the choices you have.

Summary

  • You need an account (Sign in with Apple, Google or email). We store your email address and the name you choose.
  • Your style quiz answers, your clothing photos and the outfits we create are stored on our servers so the app can work across sessions.
  • Photos are analysed by an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to recognise the item. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • The app shows optional video ads from Google AdMob. Tracking is used only if you allow it in Apple's prompt.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time in the app. We do not sell your data.

Information we collect and why

Account

Email address, the name you enter and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, a user identifier from that provider. With email sign-in we store your password only as a one-way hash. We use this to keep you signed in and to protect your account.

Style profile

Your quiz answers: gender (for clothing styles), age range, where you spend your day, dress code, preferred styles, comfort vs. style, fit, liked and avoided colours and patterns, accessory use, the look you want (optional) and the outfit cards you liked. We use them only to suggest outfits and to judge items for you. We do not ask for your height, weight or location.

Wardrobe photos

Photos you add of your clothes, and the labels created for them (category, colours, pattern, formality, seasons). Before upload the app shrinks the photo and removes its metadata (such as GPS location). Our server also creates a copy with the background removed. Photos that show a person or are not clothing are rejected and not stored.

Outfits and "Should I buy it?"

The outfits we suggest (which of your items, a short comment and tips), and the photos you take of items you are considering buying, with our assessment. These let you see your recent results.

Usage limits

How many outfits and checks you used today and whether you watched a rewarded video, so daily limits work.

AI processing

To recognise clothing and write outfit suggestions, our server sends the photo (or, for outfits, only the text labels of your items and your style profile) to Anthropic, PBC through its API. Anthropic processes this on our behalf, does not use it to train its models and deletes it after a limited retention period under its commercial terms. AI suggestions can be wrong; use your own judgement.

Ads

The app shows no banners and no full-screen ads. Only if you choose to, you can watch a video ad to get one extra outfit or check per day. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which receives information such as your IP address, device model, OS version, an advertising or vendor identifier and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. To confirm that you finished a video, Google sends our server a signed notice with your account identifier. On first launch after signing in, iOS asks whether the app may track you; if you decline, ads are not personalised. Google acts as an independent controller; see how Google uses information in ads.

Subscriptions

If a Premium subscription is offered, payments are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, Inc. processes the purchase record and your account identifier so we can unlock Premium.

Where data is stored, and for how long

Data is stored on servers operated by us or our hosting provider. We keep it while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, style profile, photos, outfits, checks and usage records are deleted from our servers immediately; copies in server backups are removed as backups are rotated.

Your choices and rights

  • Correct an item's category in the app or delete individual items.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account.
  • Change ad tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or any other request: mekaxstudio@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
