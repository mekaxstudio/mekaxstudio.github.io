WearUp

Your wardrobe, your personal stylist. Add your clothes and get an outfit for where you are going.

Take a short style quiz, add photos of your clothes and WearUp builds outfits from your own wardrobe for school, work, a date, a party or a wedding, with a short comment and styling tips. In a store, "Should I buy it?" tells you how well an item fits your style and your wardrobe.

Contact us

Found a bug or have an idea? Email mekaxstudio@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Frequently asked questions

Why was my photo rejected?

Only photos of a single wearable item are accepted: on a hanger, laid flat or a clean product photo. Photos that show a person (for example a mirror selfie), several items at once or something that is not clothing are rejected, and rejected photos are not stored.

How many outfits can I make?

One outfit and one "Should I buy it?" check per day, plus one more of each after an optional video ad. Your daily count resets at midnight (Turkey time).

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete account. Your account, style profile, wardrobe photos, outfits and checks are deleted permanently. See the Privacy Policy.

The category of an item is wrong. Can I fix it?

Yes. Open the item in your wardrobe and pick the right category; outfits use your correction from then on.
